# cute-apps

Small, focused, open-source Android apps. No ads, no accounts, nothing collected by us.

[![Sponsor](https://img.shields.io/badge/Sponsor-%E2%9D%A4-ea4aaa?logo=githubsponsors&logoColor=white)](https://github.com/sponsors/Vaib215)

| | App | What it is |
| --- | --- | --- |
| <img src="cute-reads/docs/icon.png" width="56" alt="Cute Reads icon"> | [**Cute Reads**](cute-reads/) | A calm, distraction-free e-book reader for EPUB and plain-text books, with home-screen widgets for your book and your page. |
| <img src="cute-notes/docs/icon.png" width="56" alt="Cute Notes icon"> | [**Cute Notes**](cute-notes/) | Notes you write big with your finger; your handwriting turns into text as you go. Voice and keyboard too. |
| <img src="cute-sleep/docs/icon.png" width="56" alt="Cute Sleep icon"> | [**Cute Sleep**](cute-sleep/) | A bedside clock that comes up by itself when your phone charges, like iPhone StandBy, upright or sideways. Plus clock widgets. |

Download APKs from [Releases](https://github.com/Vaib215/cute-apps/releases). Each needs Android 8.0 or newer.
